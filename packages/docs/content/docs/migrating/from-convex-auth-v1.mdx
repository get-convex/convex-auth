## How to migrate?

<Callout type="warn">WIP</Callout>

Sessions don’t carry over from v1. v2 stores its tokens under new keys in
`localStorage` (and in new cookies with Next.js) and ignores the v1 ones, so
every user has to sign in again after the upgrade.
